Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might want to know before you book. Still have a question? Call or text us at 702.720.0758.

Do you really come to me?

Yes — Coolcare Details is fully mobile. We come to your home or workplace anywhere in the Las Vegas Valley with everything needed to complete the detail on-site. You never have to drive to a shop or wait around.

Do you need me to provide power or water?

No — we bring our own. Our fully equipped setup carries its own power and water, so you don't need to provide anything. All we need is a spot to park and access to your vehicle.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Coolcare Details is fully licensed and insured, so your vehicle and property are protected while we work.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept most payment methods — credit and debit cards including American Express, Apple Pay, Venmo, Zelle, cash, and even Bitcoin. Whatever is easiest for you.

How long does a detail take?

It depends on the vehicle and the package. A Basic detail is usually a couple of hours, while a Full detail with clay bar, wax and shampoo can take several hours. Ceramic coating and paint correction take longer. We'll give you a time estimate when you book.

How much does mobile detailing cost?

We use flat package pricing that starts at $85 for a small-car Basic detail and goes up by vehicle size and package. Ceramic coating and paint correction are quoted per vehicle since they vary with condition. See packages & pricing or our services, or call for a quote.

What areas do you serve?

The entire Las Vegas Valley — including Henderson, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Spring Valley, Enterprise, Paradise, Sunrise Manor, Winchester, Whitney (East Las Vegas) and Boulder City. Not sure if you're in range? Just call or text.

Do you offer ceramic coating and paint correction?

Yes. We offer ceramic coating to lock in gloss and make your car easier to keep clean, and paint correction to remove swirls, light scratches and oxidation. Both are priced per vehicle based on condition — call or text for a quote.

How often should I detail my car in Las Vegas?

The Nevada sun and heat are hard on paint and interiors, so many drivers do a full detail every few months and a lighter refresh in between. Ceramic protection helps your car look better for longer between details.

Do you detail trucks and large SUVs?

Absolutely. We detail everything from small cars and sedans to midsize SUVs and pickups and full-size trucks and SUVs. Pricing is set by vehicle size, so you always know what to expect.

What if my vehicle is really dirty or has pet hair?

No problem — we handle heavily soiled vehicles all the time. Extreme cases like heavy pet hair, excessive grime, trash or severe stains may add to the base price, and we'll always let you know before we start.

How do I book?

Book right on our website in under a minute, or call or text us at 702-720-0758. We'll confirm your appointment and give you a time window.
