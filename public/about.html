About Coolcare

Part of the CoolCare family — mobile detailing brought to your driveway, anywhere in the Las Vegas Valley.

Serving the Las Vegas Valley for 2+ years

CoolCare Details is the auto detailing arm of the CoolCare family — run alongside our truck wash, focused on cars, SUVs, and pickups. We deliver deep interior cleaning, exterior restoration, and protection that lasts.

It's fully mobile: no need to drop your vehicle off or wait at a shop. We come to your home or workplace anywhere in the Las Vegas Valley with all the equipment and products needed to get the job done.

From a quick refresh to a complete multi-stage detail with ceramic protection, every vehicle leaves looking and feeling brand new.

Two years in and hundreds of vehicles later, we're still run by the same hands that do the work — so when you call, you're talking to the person who'll be standing in your driveway making your car shine.
