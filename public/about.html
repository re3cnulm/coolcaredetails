About Coolcare

Mobile auto detailing, brought to your driveway — anywhere in the Las Vegas valley.

Coolcare Details is a mobile detailing service built on a simple idea: getting your car detailed shouldn't cost you a day. Instead of you driving across town and waiting around a shop, we bring the whole setup to you — at home, at work, wherever you park.

Every job gets the same attention, whether it's a quick refresh on a daily commuter or a full paint decontamination and ceramic protection on a weekend build. We use quality products, take our time on the details other people skip, and treat every vehicle like it's our own.

We're proud to serve drivers all across Las Vegas and the surrounding valley — and we'd love to earn your business, and your review.

  • We Come To You

    Fully mobile. We bring the equipment, the products and the water — you keep your day.

  • Honest, Flat Pricing

    Clear package prices up front, listed right on the site. No surprises when we arrive.

  • Detail-Obsessed

    Door jambs, seat rails, headliners — the spots most washes ignore are the ones we sweat.

  • Local & Reliable

    Las Vegas born and run. Text us and you'll talk to the person doing the work.
